Privacy Policy for Vewra

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 Developer: DevHub Studios Package: com.devhubstudios.vewra

DevHub Studios built the Vewra mobile application as a free service to connect Interviewers and Interview Givers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use Vewra.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use Vewra, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account Information: Your full name, email address, and password (when registering via Email/Password). Passwords are handled securely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text or accessible to us.
  • Google Sign-In Information: If you choose "Continue with Google," we receive your basic Google profile information (name, email address, and unique user identifier) as permitted by your Google account settings.
  • Role & Profile Data: Your selected role (Interviewer or Interview Giver), unique user ID (uid), and account creation timestamp.
  • Interview & Activity Data: Any interview slots, booking details, or profile information you voluntarily submit within the app to connect with other users.

We do not collect sensitive financial information, precise GPS location, contacts, or media files without your explicit action.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Route you to the appropriate dashboard (Interviewer or Interview Giver).
  • Facilitate connections and interview scheduling between Interviewers and Interview Givers.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for advertising or marketing.

3. Third-Party Services

Vewra relies on trusted third-party infrastructure provided by Google to operate. These services may collect device and diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services – Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication – Manages user sign-in and account security.
  • Cloud Firestore – Stores user profiles and app data securely in the cloud.
  • Firebase Privacy & Security – Firebase Privacy Information

4. Data Sharing and Visibility

Because Vewra connects Interviewers and Interview Givers, basic profile details (such as your name and role) may be visible to other authenticated users within the app as needed to schedule or conduct interviews. We do not disclose your personal data to external parties unless required by law.

5. Data Security and Retention

All data transmitted between Vewra and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the service.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your Vewra account and all associated personal data stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

To request account and data deletion:
  1. Email us at vishnugupta9571@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Delete My Vewra Account".
  2. We will permanently delete your authentication record and Firestore profile data within 7 business days and confirm via email.

7. Children's Privacy

Vewra is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features to Vewra. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
